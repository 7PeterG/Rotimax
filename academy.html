ABOUT THE ACADEMY

Professional Training Since
2014

The Rotimax Training Academy provides practical, professional training in pest management and fumigation to individuals, aspiring pest-control operators and corporate clients across Nigeria.

Our programmes combine classroom learning with hands-on field training, delivered by experienced professionals who actively work in the industry. Graduates receive certification and can access a one-year mentorship programme.
