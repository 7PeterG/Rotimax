OUR STORY

Who We Are

Rotimax Integrated Services Limited is a Nigerian environmental health and pest management company providing professional pest control, fumigation, cleaning, disinfection, facility management and environmental solutions for residential, commercial and industrial clients.

We are based in Ikeja, Lagos and operate across Nigeria, delivering consistent, professional-grade services backed by trained teams, appropriate equipment and documented procedures.

Rotimax also operates a professional training academy providing practical pest management and fumigation training, certification and mentorship — helping build the next generation of pest-management professionals in Nigeria.

Our Journey

2010

Rotimax Integrated Services Limited is founded as a professional pest management and environmental services company in Lagos, Nigeria.

2014+

Launch of professional pest-management training programmes for individuals, aspiring operators and corporate clients.

2023

The Rotimax Training Academy marks a decade of professional pest-management education, certification and mentorship.
